A multi-agent travel planning application powered by Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) and Gemini 2.0. Eight specialized agents collaborate to produce a complete day-by-day itinerary — covering routes, flights, hotels, activities, weather forecasts, and budget — exportable as an Excel file.

**Agents:** Route, Flight, Hotel, Activity, Weather, Critic, Refiner, Export

**Stack:** Python, Google ADK, Gemini 2.0 Flash, Streamlit, Pandas, OpenPyXL

**Source:** [github.com/liuxhy/trip_planner_agent](https://github.com/liuxhy/trip_planner_agent)
